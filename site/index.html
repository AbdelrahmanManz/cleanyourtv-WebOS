CleanYourTV for LG webOS¶

A fast home screen for rooted LG webOS TVs. It replaces LG's Home with a dock of four apps, a top shelf and an app grid, and adds Live TV from your own playlist, an Inputs list on the Input key, picture notifications from your network, optional on-device voice recognition, and an optional debloat of LG's ads and telemetry.

What it does¶

  • Launcher. A dock of four apps, a top shelf with the focused app's name and an ambient glow, an app grid sorted by how often you use each app, an Inputs picker, a Settings shortcut and a clock. More.
  • Live TV. Plays your M3U playlist with a channel list, a programme guide (XMLTV) and number entry. It does not use LG's tuner. More.
  • Input key. The remote's Input button opens an Inputs list over the running app. More.
  • Picture notifications. A card with a photo over whatever is on screen, triggered from your network, for example by Home Assistant. More.
  • Voice (optional). Speech recognition on the TV (Moonshine), with answers from a language model through OpenRouter or from your own server, shown as text. More.
  • Debloat. Stops LG's ad and telemetry services and blocks a few ad servers. More.
  • Manager app. Sets up, updates and removes everything from the TV. More.

Everything is applied in RAM at boot. Nothing under /usr, /etc or the firmware is modified, and a reboot without the boot scripts brings LG's Home back. See What it changes on the TV.

Compatibility at a glance¶
Needs An LG webOS TV that is rooted with the Homebrew Channel, and SSH access as root for the install command.
webOS webOS 5 and newer (2020 models and later). webOS 4 and older have no systemd and an old Node.js; setup stops before it changes anything.
Tested LG OLED65G26LA on webOS 25. Other TVs are reported in Tested TVs.
Not needed A computer app, a server, a cloud account. The service runs on the TV.

This modifies a TV with root access

Read install.sh and the scripts it installs before you run them, and use the project at your own risk. The setup saves the original state, and a failed start restarts the TV with LG's Home, but nothing here is covered by a warranty.

Where to start¶

  1. Make sure the TV is rooted and has the Homebrew Channel (rootmy.tv).
  2. Follow Installing: one command from your computer, then Set up now in the app on the TV.
  3. Check the tested TVs and report yours if it is not listed.
